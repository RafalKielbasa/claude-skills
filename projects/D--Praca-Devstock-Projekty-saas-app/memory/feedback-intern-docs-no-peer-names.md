Rafał (2026-10-22, Norbert's analysis): "nie używaj bezpośrednich imion, raczej użyj frazy
w stosunku do reszty zespołu". In intern-facing material the comparison group is "reszta
zespołu": a table row "reszta zespołu" with a min–max range, percentages as ranges
("41–55%"), sums for counts ("7 PR-ów, 2 rundy"). Never a per-person row, and no "kolega A/B"
either — with two peers that identifies them anyway.

**Why:** a feedback document goes to one intern; naming peers exposes their results to a
colleague and turns a performance review into a ranking.

**How to apply:** applies to files and Discord/GitHub messages addressed to an intern. Rafał's
internal reports (e.g. `ocena-praktykantow-2026-10.md`) keep names. Peers' weaknesses never
appear in another intern's document at all, named or not. Related: [[intern-evaluation-2026-10]].
