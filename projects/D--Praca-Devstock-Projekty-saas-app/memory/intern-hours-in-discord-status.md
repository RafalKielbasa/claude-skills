Rafał (2026-10-22): interns who give their hours in the daily Discord status ("Łączny poświęcony
czas") do not have to log time as a comment on the board card. The regulation
(`Baza wiedzy/knowledge-base/praktyki/zasady-wspolpracy-praktyki.md`) still says "logujemy
komentarzem na tickecie", but Rafał's rule overrides it. Other conventions (branch naming,
`(CP-n)` in commits, demo video) still count as errors when broken.

**Why:** the 2026-10-15 report (`ocena-praktykantow-2026-10.md` in the vault) and the first
draft of Norbert's analysis flagged "no time on tickets" as a breach; Rafał corrected it —
Norbert does log hours, on Discord.

**How to apply:** never list missing ticket time logs as a finding for interns; verify hours
from the Discord statuses (no Discord tool here — ask Rafał to paste them). The vault report
from 15.10 still carries the outdated claim ("Nikt nie loguje czasu na issue") — fix only with
Rafał's consent. Related: [[intern-evaluation-2026-10]].
