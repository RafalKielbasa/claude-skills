Rafał wants to build a business of his own that is managed AI-native from day one and designed around collecting and processing information. First stated 2026-09-06 as a *physical (non-digital)* business; on 2026-09-18 he widened it: **any kind of business, provided it is AI-native and data-centred** (decision D7 of the transformation-program spec). The 2026-09-06 "physical only" constraint no longer applies.

Key constraints and intent (current as of 2026-09-19):
- AI must be present first in the process of *creating* the company (the idea-engine), then in *running* it (the flagship service of the transformation program is the seed of that operating system).
- Rafał builds the agent systems himself; agents should do everything they are good at. Robotics is not excluded, only avoided at the start for cost (2026-09-06).
- Capital: 100 000–300 000 PLN available by 2028 → `engine.config.json` `capitalCeiling` becomes 300 000 (to be set in week 0 of the program; the committed 1 500 000 is illustrative).
- Horizon: go / no-go / pivot decision by 2027-06-30 at the latest, first unit selling in 2028.
- The business is not decided. Validation starts in the sectors of `engine.config.json` (food, health, education, automotive, furniture); service and information models enter through screening seeds.
- The father's ambulance-rota business is explicitly NOT a candidate and not a testbed (2026-09-18).
- `fit-fast-food` (imported from `fast-fit/Analiza AI Fit Fast Food.md`, status `validated` since 2026-09-07) is expected to get a `no-go` verdict in cycle 1 of the program (hub does not fit the ceiling, Salad and Go precedent, no price premium); `pivot` to a hub-less micro-format is the alternative. Rafał decides.
- idea-engine gets no new features until 2027-03-28; the interview-planner extension stays deferred.
- Reframed 2026-10-22 (brainstorming on an "alternative income source"): Rafał confirmed the side income he wants is this same goal in a digital form, started earlier and small — from home at a computer, alongside his 8 h job, with AI agents doing the work the customer pays for, built on his current experience. Top criteria: scalability and room to grow; long term it should become passive or near-passive income. No money target or deadline (he has savings). Motivation: securing his family as much as possible; Devstock's difficult financial situation is only one element. Hourly freelance/consulting counts at most as a stepping stone, not the pillar. Weekly hours for it were still open. Ambition: combined income (job + this) of 50 000–100 000 PLN/month "in a few years". What he enjoyed most: cooking and building agentic systems; writing training material is now effortless for him but not what drives him. He asked not to force gastronomy × agents — focus on a scalable agent-based business in any sector. Start capital for this: max 10 000 PLN (assets: computer, tokens, his knowledge and time). Extra interest signal (not a condition): interior / garden / house design and construction — creating things in the physical world gives him great satisfaction. Deep research `reports/Skalowalny biznes na agentach AI.md` (2026-10-22) recommended a KSeF agent for accounting offices (~1 000 PLN/month) as the foundation; Rafał instead chose (2026-10-22) **an agent for quotes/estimates for micro renovation firms, sold as a cheap subscription** — passion over the report's arithmetic, decided knowingly; do not re-litigate, but design for self-serve onboarding and a channel that brings many firms without his hours. Product shape (his words): a "second brain" for a busy contractor (client records, payments paid/remaining, deadlines, calendar, delay notices to the investor); V1 = record the on-site conversation with the investor → one summary sent to both sides, who then discuss it → cost estimate from the contractor's own data. Decision 2026-10-22 ("OK" to variant A): the transformation-program flagship becomes the product's generic public engine (memory, transcript → summary, approval gate); the contractor product lives in a separate private repo; the program spec needs an amendment (domain, probably earlier information-loop cycle).

**Why:** the whole repo exists to serve this goal; without this note the fast-food analysis looks like the only project and the old "physical only" constraint would wrongly filter ideas.
