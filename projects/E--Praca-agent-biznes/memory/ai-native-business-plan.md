Rafał wants to build a business of his own that is managed AI-native from day one and designed around collecting and processing information. First stated 2026-09-06 as a *physical (non-digital)* business; on 2026-09-18 he widened it: **any kind of business, provided it is AI-native and data-centred** (decision D7 of the transformation-program spec). The 2026-09-06 "physical only" constraint no longer applies.

Key constraints and intent (current as of 2026-09-19):
- AI must be present first in the process of *creating* the company (the idea-engine), then in *running* it (the flagship service of the transformation program is the seed of that operating system).
- Rafał builds the agent systems himself; agents should do everything they are good at. Robotics is not excluded, only avoided at the start for cost (2026-09-06).
- Capital: 100 000–300 000 PLN available by 2028 → `engine.config.json` `capitalCeiling` becomes 300 000 (to be set in week 0 of the program; the committed 1 500 000 is illustrative).
- Horizon: go / no-go / pivot decision by 2027-06-30 at the latest, first unit selling in 2028.
- The business is not decided. Validation starts in the sectors of `engine.config.json` (food, health, education, automotive, furniture); service and information models enter through screening seeds.
- The father's ambulance-rota business is explicitly NOT a candidate and not a testbed (2026-09-18).
- `fit-fast-food` (imported from `fast-fit/Analiza AI Fit Fast Food.md`, status `validated` since 2026-09-07) is expected to get a `no-go` verdict in cycle 1 of the program (hub does not fit the ceiling, Salad and Go precedent, no price premium); `pivot` to a hub-less micro-format is the alternative. Rafał decides.
- idea-engine gets no new features until 2027-03-28; the interview-planner extension stays deferred.

**Why:** the whole repo exists to serve this goal; without this note the fast-food analysis looks like the only project and the old "physical only" constraint would wrongly filter ideas.
**How to apply:** treat any new idea or tool in this repo as a candidate for the validation pipeline and the future company-management system, not as a one-off; run the engine at the pace set in [[transformation-program-2026h2]] (one step per six-week cycle, Opus agents only after an explicit yes).
