Rafał wants to build a business in the physical (non-digital) world that is managed AI-native from day one. Stated 2026-09-06.

Key constraints and intent:
- AI must be present first in the process of *creating* the company, then in *running* it.
- Rafał will use his own technical skills to build the agent system; agents should do everything they are good at.
- Everything is to be designed from the start around collecting and processing information.
- Robotics is NOT an exclusion (confirmed 2026-09-06). It is avoided at the start because of cost, so it is scored as CAPEX/service risk, and concepts may plan a later robotics phase.
- The actual business is not decided. Validation starts in sectors close to Rafał: food, health, education/science, automotive, high-quality furniture production.
- First document under consideration: `fast-fit/Analiza AI Fit Fast Food.md` (feasibility of an AI-native fit fast food chain in Poland; conclusion: no kitchen robots, AI in back-office, hub-and-spoke model).

**Why:** the whole repo exists to serve this goal; without it the fast-food analysis looks like the only project.
**How to apply:** treat any new idea or tool in this repo as a candidate for the validation pipeline and the future company-management system, not as a one-off. The first deliverable is the Idea Engine (Claude Code skill `idea-engine`, JSON claims with source tiers, Node CLI); its design spec lives in the repo at `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-idea-engine-design.md`, which also lists the four gaps found in the fast food document.
